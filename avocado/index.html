A few useful details

From camera to content.

How does scanning work?

An AR Code is added to an image or video. A supported camera experience detects and decodes the marker, then connects to the encoded digital destination. The original Avocado iOS app demonstrates the mobile integration.

Why use an AR Code?

The content anchor is encoded into the media frame, enabling interactions with print and moving video without a separate static reference image for each piece of content.

What can an advertiser connect?

The original showcase includes websites, product content, coupons, rewards, sweepstakes, and interactive experiences across television, magazines, newspapers, signs, and billboards.
